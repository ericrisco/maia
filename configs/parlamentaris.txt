# Speaker whitelist for the Diari del Consell General — PLAN M1.05, decided by the PO (D-0049).
#
# WHAT THIS IS FOR. The parliamentary subcorpus teaches MAIA the **register and lexicon** of formal
# spoken Andorran Catalan: how people argue, qualify and address each other in an Andorran
# institutional setting, and which words they reach for. It feeds `registre: andorra_parlat`.
#
# WHY IT IS LONG, WHICH IS THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT A WHITELIST USUALLY MEANS. The first version had
# two names, and measuring it showed **94% of the corpus was one person** — Xavier Espot. A corpus
# like that does not teach a register, it teaches an idiolect: his fillers, his syntax, his way of
# not answering a question. That is both a data-quality failure and exactly what D7 forbids, and
# the fix is the same for both: more voices.
#
# With 32 named speakers the largest single share is ~13% and the corpus is 7x bigger
# (2.1M -> ~15M characters), from PDFs that were already downloaded. Nobody is reproducible.
#
# WHAT IT IS STILL NOT FOR (D7). Generated examples must never reproduce an identifiable person's
# voice or attribute a position to them. Note honestly where that is enforced: §3.1 records
# `speaker`, deliberately, so the corpus *can* be filtered back down to one person. What prevents
# the harm is D7 in use plus the M6.01 publication gate — not the shape of the data.
#
# SELECTION RULE, so this can be regenerated rather than argued about: every named person with
# >=40 substantive interventions (>=120 characters after cleaning) across 2019-2023.
#
# OFFICES OF THE CHAIR ARE EXCLUDED ON PURPOSE. "síndica general" has the most interventions of
# anyone (1,963) and almost all are procedural — giving the floor, calling a vote. Including them
# would refill the corpus with formulae and undo the point of widening it.
#
# NAMES MUST MATCH THE FORM PRINTED ON THE INTERVENTION, not the attendance list: the Diari prints
# "M. I. Sr. Raul Ferré Bonet" when taking attendance and "El Sr. Raul Ferré:" above what he says.
# `maia-consell-diari` warns about entries that never spoke and suggests the printed form.
#
# OFFICE ALIASES NEED A YEAR RANGE: `name [YYYY-YYYY]`. In 2021 the Diari prints "El Sr. cap de
# Govern:" where 2022 prints "El Sr. Xavier Espot:" — the same person. Unscoped, that alias would
# attribute Toni Martí's words to Espot, because the office was his before 2019.
