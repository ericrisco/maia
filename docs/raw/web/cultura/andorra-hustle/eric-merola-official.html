Ukraine: Fetal Stem Cell Pioneers is is my sixth feature documentary and a follow-up to my fourth film, The God Cells.

My previous film, The Andorra Hustle, was released worldwide in English, Spanish, and Catalan and received critical acclaim from publications including The Guardian and The Boston Globe. It is currently available to watch free on YouTube.

My first documentary, Burzynski: Cancer Is Serious Business became an international success, reaching tens of millions of viewers worldwide. The film was distributed on Netflix, PBS, The Documentary Channel, and numerous international television networks. Its impact led to the production of a follow-up film, Burzynski: Cancer Is Serious Business, Part II

My third documentary, Second Opinion: Laetrile At Sloan-Kettering tells the story of Ralph W. Moss, PhD, who exposed suppressed research on Laetrile conducted at Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center in the 1970s. The film received widespread attention, including a featured review in The New York Times.

I’m also a painter and fine artist currently holding a residency at the 18th Street Arts Center in Santa Monica.
