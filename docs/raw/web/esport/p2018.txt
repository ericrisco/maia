Andorra sent competitors to the 2018 Winter Paralympics in Pyeongchang, South Korea. They competed in para-alpine skiing. The country first went to the Winter Paralympics in 2002. When they arrived in Pyeongchang, they competed in their fifth Winter Paralympics.
Team
[edit]
The International Paralympic Committee confirmed that Andorra would compete at the 2018 Winter Paralympics.[1]

History
[edit]
The country first went to the Winter Paralympics in 2002. When they arrived in Pyeongchang, they competed in their fifth Winter Paralympics. Their best finish at the Winter Games was in 2010, when Paquita Ramirez Capitan was ninth in the women's Giant Slalom.[1]
