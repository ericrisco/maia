Maria Moles Farré
(born 8 May 2003) is an Andorran
footballer
who plays as a
midfielder
for the youth team of Spanish club
FC Levante Las Planas
and the
Andorra women's national team
.
Club career
[
edit
]
Moles is a product of
Levante Las Planas
in Spain.
International career
[
edit
]
Moles capped for Andorra at senior level in a 4–2
friendly
away win over
Liechtenstein
on 18 September 2021.
